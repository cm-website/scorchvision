App Description

ScorchVision is a cutting-edge real-time fire detection app tailored for DIY enthusiasts, developers, and smart home aficionados. By integrating with your custom Raspberry Pi camera setup, it leverages advanced on-device machine learning to detect fires instantly and send push notifications to your phone. This app is designed to provide continuous background monitoring, ensuring safety even when the app is closed. With a focus on privacy, all processing is done locally, eliminating the need for cloud services and data sharing. ScorchVision is perfect for home automation and remote safety projects, offering seamless integration with Raspberry Pi and camera modules. However, it is important to note that ScorchVision is not a certified fire alarm and should not replace professional fire safety systems. Always adhere to local fire safety regulations.
